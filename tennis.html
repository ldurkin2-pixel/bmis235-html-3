Tennis

Tennis is a sport I played often as a kid, but I picked it up again in the past year.

Over the summer, I went with my brother as his plus one to a free day at Silverwood that Baker Tilly provided for their employees. There, I met one of his good friends and his little sister, who played tennis for Whitworth. The four of us started playing tennis regularly for hours on end. I took lessons as a young girl, but I was very rusty. Now that I have gained some skill back, it was been a great way to get outside for more intensive cardio than golf. I have started to play with other friends and family as well. In Montana, where my grandmother lives, she has a tennis court on the property. We have put that to use frequently as of late. It makes me feel refreshed while getting excellent exercise outside.
