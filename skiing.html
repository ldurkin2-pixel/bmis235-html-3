Skiing

Skiing, particularly ski racing, was my main sport before golf for 10 years.

I was 18 months old the first time I skied. I refused to wear a helmet or gloves, my boots were many sizes too big, and I wore a horrible bright blue jumpsuit that was a handmedown from my brothers (also way too big).I started ski racing at Lookout Pass when I was about 4. As I made my way through the age groups, I started improving substantially. I had a natural knack for racing, and by the end of my career, I had achieved over 50 podiums. I made many special friends on the mountain who I am still close with. It also made me a better skier for life, which I am grateful for. Unfortunately, it caused two disc ruptures in my lower back, so I had to call it quits entering high school. In the end, it worked out, since it opened the door for me to try golf. Being on the mountain are some of the most memorable and best times of my life, and I hope that our world can find a solution to climate change to keep our winters around for much longer.
