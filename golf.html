Golf

The sport I play most often is golf.

I started playing golf as soon as I could walk. My mother was an avid scratch handicap golfer since she was in college. Growing up living on a golf course, it was impossible to escape it. My older brother loved it since the beginning as a toddler. Even though I love it now, it took me a long time to get there. I had never played in a single tournament until high school. I was afraid of being watched and judged by others, since I was shooting mid-to-high 90s when I was 14. However, after realizing how much fun the atmosphere was, I fell in love with it. I loved competing against myself and my own mind every day. I started to spend hours on end at the course every day. I golfed six days a week for four years straight. In winter, I used the simulators religiously. By my senior year, I was shooting under par and won 5 tournaments. However, I never cared much for competing against other people. I loved challenging myself and my mental game. It taught me how to remain calm under pressure and focus on myself rather than what other people are doing. I am proud of the discipline I had to have to improve at golf for years. I never knew that I would come to love golf as much as I do, but I am very thankful for the lessons it taught me.
